# ELI5 and 30-second pitch

A note app is useful when you come back to use what you saved, not just when you sign up. This prototype lets someone turn a short meeting note into tasks and see a reason to come back.

The growth side compares two fake onboarding journeys. It checks whether more people get value *again*, not just whether more people start a trial. It also looks at refunds and money per user. A result can look good overall but be caused by different kinds of customers entering each group. The demo makes that mistake visible and refuses to scale it.

Evernote already has notes, tasks and templates. This is a proposed sequence and measurement experiment. It is not a claim that those features are missing or that the current flow is broken. No real Evernote accounts, reminders, billing or customer data are connected.

**30-second pitch:** "For Bending Spoons' Growth manager role, I built an Evernote habit experiment. You turn a meeting note into tasks, see a repeat-value journey, then inspect a synthetic growth test. It checks retention, conversion and net revenue, and it catches a result caused by acquisition mix rather than the product. It's a working user flow and a disciplined scale decision, not just a dashboard that says conversion went up."
