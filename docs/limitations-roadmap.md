# Limitations and v2 roadmap

## Limitations
- Synthetic data. One user per browser in the preview. A simulated clock.
- No real Evernote integration, reminders, billing or accounts.
- Descriptive intervals only. Fixed demo thresholds. No power calculation.
- localStorage is not durable, shared or secure.
- Help-centre and Medium pages returned 403 to automated fetches, so the brand evidence comes from evernote.com and bendingspoons.com.

## v2 (each needs approval)
- An approved analytics adapter (read-only, aggregated) in place of CSV upload.
- Longer retention and cohort curves (day 30/60), and repeat-value → paid-retention validation.
- Power and sample-size calculator from real baseline rates.
- Stratified assignment by acquisition source.
- Evidence-backed onboarding variants from the discovery plan.
